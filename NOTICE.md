City/place coordinates and primary-county attributes are derived from the
user-supplied SimpleMaps U.S. Cities database. Visible SimpleMaps attribution is
retained in the interface. Review the source-data license before redistributing
the raw cities file contained only in the complete reproduction package.

The Census urban-area file is used as a 2010 Census urban-area layer, not as a
municipal boundary layer. County values derived from a matched city's primary
county remain marked as inferred.
