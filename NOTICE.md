# Notice

This dashboard is an exploratory research and educational tool. It is not an official HUD system and does not constitute a legal, privacy, accounting, or program-compliance determination.

Narrative excerpts underwent automated activity-aware address screening. Potential single-family or ambiguous addresses were redacted; addresses retained in infrastructure/public-facility or clearly multifamily/rental contexts are highlighted. Automated screening may produce false positives or omissions and does not replace authorized human or legal review.
